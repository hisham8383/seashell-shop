Our Story

A shop spun from moonlight, salt air, and gold leaf

Nacré began with a single shell held up to the light — the way it scattered gold across the walls, soft and unrepeatable. We chase that same feeling in everything we make: each hanging and chandelier is gathered, cleaned, and strung by hand from shells chosen for how they catch a room's light.

Ours is a world of warm browns, blush pinks, and gilded edges — bohemian in spirit, refined in detail. Every piece carries a little of the sea's own magic into your home.
